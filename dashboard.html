Today's Digest

**Sports Digest — Tuesday** The NFL trade deadline dominated the day's news. The Las Vegas Raiders dealt quarterback Kirk Cousins to the Minnesota Vikings, clearing the way for rookie Fernando Mendoza to make his first career start against the Seahawks in Week 10. The move drew mixed reviews — analysts largely praised Las Vegas for pivoting to its future, while Mendoza received a brief message of encouragement from Cousins himself. Elsewhere, the Kansas City Chiefs acquired wide receiver Josh Downs from the Colts, and the San Francisco 49ers landed Keenan Allen, also from Indianapolis, bolstering their Super Bowl aspirations. The Colts, meanwhile, held onto running back Jonathan Taylor despite reportedly fielding multiple second-round pick offers. The Arizona Cardinals suffered a significant blow, placing quarterback Gardner Minshew on IR with a feared torn UCL. Miami's Malik Willis also landed on IR with a groin injury, prompting the Dolphins to work out replacement options. On the NHL side, Vezina Trophy-winning goaltender Connor Hellebuyck revealed he requested a trade from Winnipeg due to concerns about his family and career uncertainty — a notable admission. Elsewhere, Calgary's Jacob Markstrom-turned-Edmonton Oilers netminder Jonas Levi improved to 8-1 after a 3-1 win over the Rangers. Evander Kane signed with KHL club Sibir Novosibirsk after 16 NHL seasons, and the PWHL unveiled names and logos for its four new expansion teams. In MLB, the White Sox's Will Venable and the Brewers' Pat Murphy won Manager of the Year honors.
