**Sports Digest — Tuesday, October 27** The Indianapolis Colts continued a dramatic trade deadline fire sale, dealing two veteran starters in a single day. Pro Bowl guard Quenton Nelson was sent to the Seattle Seahawks, while three-time Pro Bowl defensive tackle DeForest Buckner was traded back to the San Francisco 49ers — the team that originally drafted him — in exchange for a fourth-round pick. The Jets stand to benefit long-term, holding conditional draft capital tied to the Colts' rebuilding trajectory. Elsewhere in the NFL, the Cowboys picked up a bounce-back road win in Philadelphia, with the running game leading the way. Dallas's defense also made enough plays to quiet a struggling Eagles offense. On the injury front, Micah Parsons is reportedly nearing a return, though his team designation drew some confusion in reporting — the Cowboys linebacker has been sidelined for several weeks. The Broncos are preparing for a pivotal Week 8 matchup against the Kansas City Chiefs, who are reeling after a blowout loss to Seattle on Sunday Night Football. In the NHL, Connor Hellebuyck made his New Jersey Devils debut Tuesday night in Dallas, his first game action since being suspended and traded by the Winnipeg Jets. Vegas Golden Knights starter Carter Hart left morning skate with an apparent lower-body injury, with Adin Hill stepping in to start against Ottawa. In baseball, the Milwaukee Brewers lead the Tampa Bay Rays 3-0 in the World Series, with Game 4 set for Tuesday night in Tampa.
