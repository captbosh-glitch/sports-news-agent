**Sports Digest — November 28, 2026** **NFL** The Pittsburgh Steelers dominated the Denver Broncos 27-5 in Black Friday's marquee NFL game, with their defense scoring two touchdowns and Bo Nix turning the ball over three times in what many are calling his worst professional performance.
