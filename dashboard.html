**Sports Digest — Friday, October 2, 2026** The NHL's opening week delivered several standout moments. Rangers goaltender Igor Shesterkin made history Thursday, becoming the first New York netminder — and first NHL goalie since March 2025 — to score a goal, lifting the puck into an empty net in a 5-1 win over Tampa Bay. In Florida, Panthers captain Aleksander Barkov left Thursday's game with a leg injury after an awkward fall; the team is expected to provide an update Friday, making it the most significant injury concern of the young season. Edmonton dominated Vancouver 9-7 in a high-scoring rematch, with Connor McDavid posting five points and Evan Bouchard adding five of his own. San Jose topped Florida 4-3 in overtime, with defenseman Luca Cagnoni scoring the first NHL goal of his career as the game-winner. Anaheim locked up 22-year-old defenseman Tristan Luneau to a six-year, $43.2 million extension after just 14 NHL games, a notable front-office bet on an injury-prone but highly regarded prospect. In the NFL, Deshaun Watson rallied Cleveland to a last-second field goal to beat Pittsburgh on Thursday Night Football, while the Steelers dealt cornerback Joey Porter Jr. to Dallas just hours before kickoff. Tony Romo and CBS officially parted ways, following his OWI arrest in July. Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels has been ruled out for Sunday's London game against the Colts, with Marcus Mariota set to start. In MLB, the Braves advanced in the playoffs while the Phillies were eliminated Thursday night.
