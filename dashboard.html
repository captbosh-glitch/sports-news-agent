**Sports Digest — October 30, 2026** The biggest story in hockey is Dylan Larkin's suspension by the Detroit Red Wings after his agent informed the team he will no longer play for the franchise. Multiple teams are circling, including the Minnesota Wild and St. Louis Blues, though Larkin has not approved either as a destination.
