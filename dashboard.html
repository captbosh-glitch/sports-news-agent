Today's Digest

**Dylan Larkin made his Montreal debut Monday night but couldn't prevent a 2-1 loss to the Los Angeles Kings**, as Darcy Kuemper stopped 29 shots and Alex Laferriere scored what may be the goal of the season — a between-the-legs finish. Larkin, acquired from Detroit in a blockbuster trade, received a rousing ovation from the Bell Centre crowd and contributed an assist. Elsewhere in the NHL, the Ottawa Senators extended their Atlantic Division lead to three points with a 2-1 win over Calgary behind Linus Ullmark's 34 saves, the Toronto Maple Leafs ended a nine-game skid by rallying past the Islanders 4-2 on Gavin McKenna's go-ahead goal, and Gabriel Vilardi recorded his second career hat trick in Winnipeg's 4-2 victory over Utah. Tampa Bay lost forward Conor Geekie for four to five months after shoulder surgery. Tonight, Connor McDavid and the Oilers host the Golden Knights with Ryan Nugent-Hopkins returning from injury. In the NFL, the Washington Commanders stunned the Cincinnati Bengals 22-20 on Monday Night Football, with Jayden Daniels engineering a 64-yard drive in the final 34 seconds to set up a walk-off field goal. The loss hurts Cincinnati's AFC playoff standing. Looking ahead, Dallas Cowboys pass rusher **Micah Parsons** has been activated off the PUP list and is set to make his season debut Wednesday against the Rams after nearly a year recovering from a torn ACL. The Bills-Chiefs Thanksgiving night matchup carries major AFC seeding implications. In MLB, the Cubs are aggressively rebuilding, signing pitcher Trevor Rogers (five years, $120M) and Japanese slugger Teruaki Sato (six years, $120M), while the Orioles landed ALCS MVP Freddy Peralta on a five-year, $125 million deal.
