Thursday's biggest NHL story was Vegas's stunning comeback: the Golden Knights erased a three-goal deficit to beat Toronto 4-3 in a shootout, with rookie Trevor Connelly scoring the winner and Jack Eichel extending his point streak to five games. Colorado also impressed, as Cale Makar (two goals, one assist) and Nathan MacKinnon (one goal, two assists) led the Avalanche to a 7-3 rout of the winless Flames — Colorado's 23 goals through four games is the NHL's best scoring start in 32 years. Frank Nazar will face a Player Safety hearing for his hit on Horvat. In the MLB playoffs, Cleveland forced an ALDS Game 5 against Chicago with a sixth-inning comeback in Game 4.
