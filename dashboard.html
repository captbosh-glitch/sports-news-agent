**NFL Week 6 Digest** Kansas City remained the AFC's dominant force, improving to 5-0 with a routine win over the Chargers, who dropped to 0-6. The game was marked by Chiefs CB Christian Roland-Wallace being ejected after throwing a punch, and Chargers WR Ladd McConkey exiting early with a concussion. The Bills also stayed near the top of the conference, holding off the Raiders 33-30 behind Josh Allen's 255-yard, two-touchdown performance. Elsewhere, the Browns edged the Ravens on a last-second field goal to claim first place in the AFC North, with Lamar Jackson sidelined. Caleb Williams made a strong return from injury for Chicago, completing 30 of 36 passes for 305 yards in a Bears win over Atlanta. The Cardinals stunned the Rams 18-16 — aided by a controversial penalty — dropping Los Angeles to 2-4 and raising serious playoff concerns. Rams WR Davante Adams also left with a non-contact injury. The Colts fell at home to the previously winless Titans, with Daniel Jones throwing two interceptions, intensifying calls for a front-office overhaul. **NHL Roundup** The Panthers recorded a historic night, with Carter Verhaeghe and Sam Bennett each recording natural hat tricks in an eight-goal rout of Seattle. Philadelphia ended a franchise-record nine-game losing streak, defeating Ottawa 3-1.
