**NHL Training Camps Dominate the Day** The biggest NHL stories as camps open center on injuries and contract news. Detroit's Dylan Larkin will not skate when Red Wings training camp begins Thursday, sidelined by an upper-body injury suffered during off-ice training this week. His situation is complicated further by an outstanding trade request, with the franchise's interim GM ruling out a full rebuild. In Carolina, Russian defenseman Alexander Nikishin is refusing to report to Hurricanes camp amid a contract stalemate and swirling trade rumors. On the contract front, Anaheim locked up restricted free agent Cutter Gauthier on a six-year, $81 million extension — the most prominent RFA deal still outstanding heading into camp. The New Jersey Devils signed Luke Evangelista to a five-year, $36.25 million deal two weeks after acquiring him from Nashville, while the Capitals secured former Panthers pick Justin Sourdif on an eight-year pact. The Vikings placed running back Jordan Mason on IR with a fractured thumb. **MLB** Pete Alonso hit his 300th career home run against his former Mets, a game-tying ninth-inning blast that fueled the Orioles to an extra-inning win, keeping Baltimore alive in the AL wild-card race.
