Today's Digest

The NFL's biggest story entering Week 7 is a wave of significant injuries and roster moves. Rams wide receiver Davante Adams will miss six to ten weeks with a ruptured hamstring suffered in Sunday's loss to the Cardinals, compounding an already difficult stretch for Los Angeles — defensive tackles Poona Ford (season-ending IR) and Kobie Turner are also sidelined, and newly signed kicker Blake Grupe landed on IR with a torn ACL. In Philadelphia, Eagles quarterback Jalen Hurts is reportedly out for the season with blood clots in his lungs, a major development in the NFC. The Colts also fired general manager Chris Ballard following a rough stretch. Elsewhere in the NFL, the Giants face a quarterback decision between Jameis Winston and Tommy McCarthy ahead of Week 7, while the Cowboys remain in crisis at 2-4 despite ownership backing coach Brian Schottenheimer. Chicago's Caleb Williams is clear of the injury report for Thursday's game against the Patriots, who themselves are managing multiple limitations including key receivers. Seattle's Zach Charbonnet is a game-time question for Sunday Night Football. In the NHL, the Winnipeg Jets face a critical decision on goaltender Connor Hellebuyck — suspended since failing to report to training camp — with a Friday deadline looming on a potential trade or reinstatement. The Jets also recalled top prospect Brayden Yager from the AHL after a nine-point run in six games. Rasmus Dahlin is nearing a return for Buffalo, and the Rangers signed veteran Luke Glendening on a PTO to address center depth.
