**Sports Digest — September 7, 2026** The NFL regular season is one week away, and injury news is already shaping Week 1 storylines. The Cowboys took a significant hit as starting left guard Tyler Smith will undergo thumb surgery and land on injured reserve, sidelining him 4–6 weeks — a notable blow heading into their opener against the Giants, who are already dealing with the release of longtime receiver Darius Slayton after seven seasons. Meanwhile, the Falcons have named Tua Tagovailoa their starting quarterback, and the 49ers will be without rotational defensive lineman Sam Okuayinonu for much of the season after he suffered a broken foot before the team's flight to Australia for Thursday's opener against the Rams. Rams DE Myles Garrett returned to practice and is expected to play. In Seattle, the Seahawks are thin at safety entering their season opener against New England, with Nick Emmanwori still limited and Ty Okada unable to practice. The Bengals received better news, with Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins back at practice and trending toward availability. On a somber note, Matt Suhey — fullback and close friend of Walter Payton on the Super Bowl XX champion 1985 Bears — passed away at age 68. In the NHL, the Red Wings' GM search continues after Detroit Tigers executive Jeff Greenberg withdrew from consideration. NHL Network ranked goaltender Ilya Sorokin among the league's top players ahead of the 2026–27 season, with Artemi Panarin and William Nylander also featured in the top 40.
