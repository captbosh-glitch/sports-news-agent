**Sports Digest — Monday, November 16** Two significant NFL injuries are dominating Monday's conversation. Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson tore his ACL — and possibly suffered additional knee damage — while celebrating a sack, ending his season in one of the more painful ironies of the year. Meanwhile, the Patriots fear quarterback Drake Maye sustained a fractured ankle that could sideline him for the remainder of the season, leaving New England scrambling for replacement options. Elsewhere in the NFL, Joe Burrow threw for 409 yards and four touchdowns in Cincinnati's 38-18 dismantling of Pittsburgh, vaulting him ahead of Josh Allen as the MVP betting favorite. San Francisco improved to 8-1 with a wild 46-40 victory over Dallas, piling up 584 yards and further deepening the Cowboys' defensive crisis. Buffalo dominated the Jets behind James Cook III's 157 rushing yards, while Kansas City's Patrick Mahomes threw five touchdowns against Atlanta. In the NHL, Connor McDavid tied Wayne Gretzky's 42-year-old record for fastest to 40 assists in a season, reaching the mark in just 22 games as Edmonton beat Philadelphia 5-2. Vancouver snapped a seven-game losing streak with a convincing 6-3 win over Vegas, while Anaheim's Cutter Gauthier extended his goal streak to nine games — the NHL's longest in 29 years — in a 6-1 rout of Calgary. Colorado received more bad news, with Nathan MacKinnon out at least a month and Cale Makar sidelined approximately two weeks.
