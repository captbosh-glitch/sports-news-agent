**Sports Digest — Week of October 24** The NHL's biggest story is the blockbuster trade sending Connor Hellebuyck from the Winnipeg Jets to the New Jersey Devils. The Hart and Vezina Trophy-winning goaltender, who backstopped the U.S. to Olympic gold earlier this year, ends his entire 11-year tenure in Winnipeg in exchange for a package of players, prospects, and picks that includes veteran center Nick Bjugstad. The move immediately strengthens an already loaded Metropolitan Division and positions New Jersey as a legitimate Stanley Cup contender. On the ice Friday night, Nathan MacKinnon posted a goal and three assists to lead Colorado past Tampa Bay 6-3, snapping a two-game skid. In the other notable result, Montreal defeated Chicago 4-1 behind two goals from Jake Evans. In the NFL, the key injury story heading into Week 7 is Lamar Jackson listed as questionable with an ankle issue for Baltimore's matchup with Cincinnati. The Giants also lost linebacker Tremaine Edmunds for the remainder of the season with a shoulder injury. On a lighter note, Saquon Barkley returned to limited practice for Philadelphia ahead of Monday night's Cowboys-Eagles matchup. In baseball, the World Series opens with the Milwaukee Brewers facing the Tampa Bay Rays. Milwaukee enters fresh off winning eight of their last nine games, with Brandon Woodruff making the roster after returning from a lengthy shoulder injury.
